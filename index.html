Block Cosmos Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Block Cosmos ("the Game") handles information when you use the game. It should be read together with the actual features and services included in the version of the Game you install.
Effective date: October 23, 2026 Last updated: October 23, 2026

1. Information Collection and Use

Block Cosmos is designed to operate without collecting personal information directly from players. The Game does not ask you to provide your name, email address, telephone number, precise location, or other personal details to play.

The Game may store non-personal game state locally on your device, such as high scores, game progress, selected visual theme, preferences, and settings. This information is used to maintain your game experience and is not, by itself, intended to identify you.

If a future version introduces online leaderboards, analytics, advertising, account sign-in, cloud saves, or other third-party services, those services may process additional information. This policy must be updated to accurately explain those services before they are enabled.

2. Data Storage and Security

Locally stored game data remains on your device unless a feature explicitly sends it elsewhere. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage may remove locally saved progress and preferences.

No method of electronic storage is completely secure. We aim to minimize the information handled by the Game and avoid collecting personal information unnecessarily.

3. Third-Party Services

This policy assumes that the Game does not include third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or other services that collect information from players. Google Play may process information under Google's own terms and privacy policies when you download or update the Game.

If third-party SDKs or online services are added to the Game, their data practices, applicable privacy policies, and any required disclosures or consent mechanisms must be reflected here.

4. Children's Privacy

Block Cosmos does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The Game is designed to minimize data collection and, under the described configuration, stores game state locally on the device.

If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child under 13 without legally required consent, we will take appropriate steps to delete that information. Parents or guardians who have questions or concerns may contact us using the details below.

This statement does not replace any additional requirements that may apply under COPPA, Google Play's Families policies, or other children's privacy laws. The Game's actual audience, SDKs, and data practices must be reviewed before publication.

5. Your Choices and Data Deletion

You can generally remove locally stored game data by clearing the Game's storage or uninstalling it. The precise result depends on your device and any backup features you have enabled.
